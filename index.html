● About

Writing with purpose.

I'm a senior Multimedia Journalism student at Bob Jones University with experience in newspaper reporting, broadcast journalism, and advertising. My background includes writing for The Collegian and The (Seneca) Journal, interning with WYFF News 4, and contributing to an integrated advertising campaign through the National Student Advertising Competition.

I'm passionate about creating clear, engaging content that connects with audiences. Whether I'm reporting a story, developing copy, or collaborating on a creative project, I strive to communicate with accuracy, purpose, and creativity.

● Approach

Every project starts with the same question.

Great communication starts with understanding people. Whether I'm reporting on a local sporting event or developing an advertising campaign, I begin by asking the same question:

“Who is this for?”

My approach combines the curiosity and accuracy of journalism with the strategic thinking of marketing. Every project begins with research, planning, and a commitment to communicating with clarity and purpose.

From newspapers to advertising campaigns, I strive to create content that is authentic and audience-focused.

● Goals

Communicating with integrity, truth, and excellence.

My goal is to use my writing and communication skills to create meaningful content that informs, inspires, and serves others. As a Christian, I believe communication is an opportunity to reflect integrity, truth, and excellence in every project I undertake.

Whether through journalism, copywriting, or strategic communications, I hope to use the talents God has given me to make a positive impact while helping organizations connect with their audiences in authentic and effective ways.
